Cup marker: how it could hold on

Sketches, not to scale. Grey is the cup, blue is the printed part.

Today: slot across the rim

  • The name's weight sits off to one side, held by 3 mm.
  • It can rock sideways along the rim, so it works loose. You are right.

A. Saddle along the rim Recommended

  • A curved groove grips 25 mm or more of rim, so it cannot rock.
  • The name sits on top, and you read it from above.
  • You set Cup diameter (default 80 mm) and Rim thickness.
  • Prints upside down with no supports. Letters sit flush in the face rather than raised.

B. Sign hooked over the rim

  • Reads from the side, like a label on the cup. This is the nicest to look at.
  • Must print standing on its side, so small letters come out rougher.
  • Colour changes on every layer: slow, with more filament waste.

C. Clip on the handle

  • Prints flat with raised letters, the simplest of the three to build.
  • Mugs only. It does nothing for a cup without a handle.
  • You set the handle's size, because handles vary a lot.
