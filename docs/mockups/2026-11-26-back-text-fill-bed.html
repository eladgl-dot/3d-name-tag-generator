Candle back text, Fill the bed, 3MF objects

Mockup, 26 Nov 2026. Candles at 4 px = 1 mm, width 16 mm. Blue = base, yellow = name and flame inlay, red = back text (filament 3, any colour you pick in Bambu).

1. Candle with back text "חנוכה שמח"

The back text is longer than the name "נוי", so it sets the body length (here 66 mm instead of 45). On the bed the back text lies mirrored (face down); turn the candle over and it reads normally, as in B. Empty "Back text" = the candle exactly as today.

4. Arrange menu: "Fill the bed", 40 candles on a 256 × 256 bed

Arrange on the plate

"Fill the bed" is new, first in the list. "Columns" is the current "Fit plate".

40 candles · 2 beds · 36 on bed 1, 4 on bed 2

Tags are turned 90° where that fits more on the bed. In the 3MF, bed 1 and bed 2 become Bambu plates 1 and 2. The red "Does not fit the plate" warning is not shown for this shape (only when one tag is bigger than the bed).
