Hanukkah candle keychain

Mockup, 22 Nov 2026. Shapes and sizes are drawn to scale (4 px = 1 mm). Text lengths are estimates; the real app measures them from the font.

1. Front view: how it sizes for short and long names

Blue = base colour (2 mm). Yellow = raised colour (1 mm on top): the name and the big flame inlay. Body length = name + 6 mm at each end, never shorter than 45 mm, never longer than 85 mm. Past 85 mm the letters and the candle shrink together (here to about 8.5 mm letters, floor 7 mm).
