How to print

In two colours

  1. Click Download 3MF.
  2. Double-click the file to open it in Bambu Studio.
  3. Base and Letters come in as two parts of one object. Choose a filament for each.
  4. Slice and print.

Good to know

  • An STL has no colours. Use 3MF to keep the two parts.
  • The colours on this page are for the preview only. The real ones are the filaments you pick.
  • The grams shown assume solid PETG, so they are an upper limit.
  • A cup marker appears upside down and mirrored, because it prints name side down with no supports. Do not flip it.

Your data

Names, settings and uploaded fonts stay in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
